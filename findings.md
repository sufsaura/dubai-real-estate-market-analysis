# Dubai Real Estate Market Analysis — Key Findings

## 1. Luxury community consistency
Bulgari Resort ranks #1 in price/sqft across all three listing types (off-plan, 
rental, secondary sale) — a consistent luxury premium regardless of transaction type.

## 2. Off-plan activity signals market maturity
Jumeirah Bay Island ranks in the top 3 for rental and resale price, but doesn't 
appear in the off-plan top 10 — suggesting the island is fully built out with 
minimal new development remaining.

## 3. Metro proximity is a weak price driver
Initial analysis showed distant listings (20+ min from metro) had inflated average 
prices, but this was a confound: Dubai's ultra-luxury island/villa communities are 
geographically isolated from metro lines. After excluding them, no clean 
"closer = pricier" relationship emerged — suggesting Dubai buyers prioritize 
community prestige over transit access.

## 4. Price growth decoupled from mortgage rates
Secondary market prices rose ~82% from 2020-2023 despite mortgage rates climbing 
from ~2% to ~6.9% — likely driven by cash/foreign-investor demand rather than 
local financing costs. From 2024-2026, prices plateaued even as rates eased, 
suggesting the recent slowdown reflects market saturation, not rate sensitivity.
